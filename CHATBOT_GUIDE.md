```dart

```

```
                    ↓
          [Today, Weekly, Monthly, All-time]

```

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### For Best Results:

1. **Be Specific:**
   ✅ "আজকের food expense কত?"
   ❌ "খরচ?" (too vague)

2. **Use Keywords:**
   ✅ "এই মাসের total"
   ✅ "সপ্তাহের খরচ"
   ✅ "food category analysis"

3. **Mix Languages:**
   ✅ Bangla + English works great!
   ✅ "আজ কত spent?"

4. **Try Quick Replies:**
   - Tap pre-defined buttons for common queries
   - Faster than typing

5. **Ask Follow-ups:**
   - "budget advice দাও"
   - "আরো tips?"
   - "detailed breakdown দাও"

### Common Patterns:
```
✅ Good: "আজকের খরচ category-wise দেখাও"
✅ Good: "এই মাসে food এ কত গেছে?"
✅ Good: "গত মাসের সাথে compare করো"

❌ Too vague: "কিছু বলো"
❌ Too complex: "আগামী বছর আমার savings কত হবে?"
❌ Not supported: "investment করা উচিত কোথায়?"
```

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Bot Not Responding?
- ✅ Check internet connection (not required, but good to have)
- ✅ Ensure you have expense data
- ✅ Try rephrasing your question

- ✅ Use specific keywords
- ✅ Try English or Bangla separately

- ✅ Use date-specific queries ("আজকের", "মাসের")

- ✅ Add some expenses first
- ✅ Check filter settings in app
- ✅ Verify date range
- ✅ Refresh expense list
